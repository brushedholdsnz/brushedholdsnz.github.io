01 / The approach

Keep the work connected.
Keep control.

The design aims to let context and capabilities support your work across sessions, models and tools, with clear rules for access and actions.

01

Recover the context.

Retrieve knowledge and artifacts with provenance, without depending on a single conversation or provider.

02

Select the resources.

Consider quality, cost and context within privacy, residency and resource constraints.

03

Coordinate within limits.

Distinguish the request, the authorization and the outcome, with reviewable evidence and explicit responsibilities.

02 / Project foundation

Collaboration and intelligence.
With governance.

PROPOSED MISSION

Connect people, artificial intelligence agents, knowledge and tools through a modular, interoperable and secure ecosystem that enables coordinated collaboration and responsible automation, with human control, verifiable permissions and operational traceability.

PROPOSED VISION

Establish Mycel-IO as an adaptable infrastructure for collaboration and distributed intelligence, able to integrate different models, providers, devices and services without depending on a single one, and to offer shared knowledge and capabilities with governance across organizations and contexts.

Mission and vision are drawn from the project’s Master Operations Manual, preserving their status as proposals. They describe a direction, not proof that every capability is available.

03 / Design principles

Connection also means
knowing the boundaries.

A request is not a permission.
An outcome needs evidence.

01 / Identity

Know who is requesting and which resource is involved.

02 / Permission

Evaluate authority and the applicable restrictions.

03 / Action

Distinguish the task, the attempt and its effect.

04 / Outcome

Preserve the verified state and its provenance.

These are principles of the contractual design. An uncertain outcome requires reconciliation; memory, recommendations or an agent’s presence do not grant authority on their own.
