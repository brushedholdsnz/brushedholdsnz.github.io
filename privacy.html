Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

This policy describes how the privately operated applications documented on this site (currently: rclone, used for personal Google Drive backups) handle data.

1. Operator

These applications are operated by a single individual for personal use. They are not a commercial service and are not made available to the general public.

2. What data is accessed

The application requests the Google Drive scope in order to read the operator's own files and folders and to upload copies of them to the same Google Drive account. Access is granted by the account owner through Google's standard OAuth consent flow, and the credential can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page.

3. How the data is used

Data is used exclusively to perform the backup: reading local files and copying them to the operator's own Google Drive. No automated analysis, profiling, advertising or resale of data takes place.

4. Where data is stored

Backups are stored in the operator's own Google Drive account. OAuth credentials are stored locally on the operator's own device, in a local configuration file that is not shared.

5. Sharing with third parties

No data is sold, rented or shared with third parties. The only external service involved is Google's own API, used to store the backups.

6. Retention and deletion

Backups can be deleted at any time by the operator from Google Drive. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

7. Google API Services User Data Policy

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

8. Contact
